  • One tap to call you, message you or get directions
  • Loads in seconds on a phone with two bars of signal

Reduce no-shows

An empty slot you could have sold twice over.

The problem

Two people don’t turn up on Saturday. No call, no message. That’s half a day you could have sold, and you’d already turned other people away to keep those slots free.

The solution

Most people don’t skip on purpose. They forget, or something came up and telling you felt awkward. So I set up reminders that land before they forget, and make moving the appointment easier than ignoring it. You stop chasing, and the slot gets refilled instead of wasted.

  • A reminder the day before, and one a couple of hours ahead
  • One tap to confirm, or to move it, so you find out early
  • Tomorrow’s list on your phone without asking anyone

Stay organised

Everything you know about your customers, in one place.

The problem

Everything your team knows about your customers lives in their heads and in their own notebooks. When someone leaves, it leaves with them, and the next person starts from nothing.

The solution

One place where each customer’s history lives, including what you’ve done for them and what to watch out for next time. Built around the way your team already works, so nobody has to change their day to keep it up to date.

  • Works on the tablet or phone you already carry around
  • A customer’s whole history in one tap, not five apps
  • New staff pick up where the last person left off
The proof

La Forge, gym

Their coaches kept everything in their heads and in personal notebooks. I built them a web app they log into, with every customer and their progress in one place. Each person has a profile with their background, old injuries and anything else worth knowing, and the app uses it to pick the workout and warm-up that suits them best, so nobody gets handed something their shoulder or knee can’t take.

  • Every coach, same pageone login, every customer’s history
  • Injuries come firstworkouts are picked around them, not despite them
  • Progress you can seetracked over time instead of remembered
